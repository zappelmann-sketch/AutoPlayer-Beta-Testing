# AutoPlayer – Your collection. Reimagined.

AutoPlayer plays your own MP3s – offline, in the car with Android Auto, with retro players, games and five Vumikio Pets that react to your music.

## At a glance

- **Android Auto:** Your music on the car's display – with voice control.
- **119 voice commands:** Hands-free control, in 26 languages.
- **Octo dances along:** Eight tentacles, eight frequency bands.
- **Offline:** Your files, your device. No streaming needed.

## Cassette, jukebox, vinyl. Your player has style.

Switch the look with one tap: cassette deck, jukebox, DJ turntable, reel-to-reel or hologram.

## Big numbers. Honestly counted.

- **26 Languages** – App, manual and voice commands
- **119 Voice commands** – hands-free, even in the car
- **5 Vumikio Pets** – that react to your music
- **10 Levels** – in Surf the Beat
- **2,315 automated tests** – run before every new version
- **10–17 Android versions** – from Android 10 to Android 17

## Surf the Beat. Your song becomes a tunnel.

A Vumikio Pet surfs through your music. The tunnel pulses to the beat – ten levels, a boss run and the giant wave.

## At home in your car. Android Auto included.

- Voice control
- Works without internet
- Android 10 to 17

## Bring your music back to the car.

For Android 10 to 17. Coming soon to Google Play.
